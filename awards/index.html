Hack In The Box 2019 – AI Competition – Finalist 2019
Hack in The Box
Hack in the Box (HITB) AI competition is a two-month competition where companies and universities compete to build an offensive security project that utilizes Artificial Intelligence in performing target exploitation. Our team reached the finals for our project, Shennina, a fully automated host exploitation and post-exploitation tool that uses AI to predict the best exploit against the target.
https://aichallenge.cyberweek.ae/

Pwnie Awards Nomination - Best Client-side Bug of 2015 2015
Pwnie Awards
The finding of CVE-2014-9414, W3 Total Cache CSRF that leads to total defacements of websites has been nominated for the best client-side bug of 2015 in the Pwnie Awards. The award is given to the person who discovered or exploited the most technically sophisticated and interesting client-side bug.
http://pwnies.com
